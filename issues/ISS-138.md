## Description
The Phase 7 roadmap still lists explicit confidence and ambiguity as unfinished. ISS-070 already added complete Subject Alignment selection evidence, including `unique`, `tied`, and `not_assessed` ambiguity plus nullable uncalibrated confidence. Interpretation uncertainty is intentionally represented through `computed_relation.status: indeterminate`, Diagnostic references, and limited coverage rather than an invented probability.

Reconcile the item, add aggregate canonical-example coverage for every accepted uncertainty state, and avoid introducing a second confidence model.

## Design constraints
- Every current producer alignment continues to emit complete selection evidence.
- `unique` remains local score uniqueness, not authoritative identity.
- `tied` remains deterministic selection among equal local evidence, not equality.
- `not_assessed` remains distinct from unique and tied.
- Confidence remains null with `confidence_status: not_calibrated` until a separate calibration task exists.
- Computed interpretation uncertainty uses `indeterminate`, nonempty resolving Diagnostic IDs, and limited coverage; do not add numeric interpretation confidence.
- Do not change Schema `1.43`, module version, report bytes, or existing uncertainty semantics.
