## Description
The engine currently retains `stroke` and `stroke-width` as mostly independent authored facts, while line caps, joins, miter limits, dash patterns, dash offsets, and `vector-effect` remain unsupported. It cannot distinguish equivalent stroke spellings from real used-geometry changes, identify when a stroke property has no painted effect, or conservatively localize the painted expansion around a shape.

## Design
Add a project-owned stroke used-geometry adapter following [SVG 2 Painting](https://www.w3.org/TR/SVG2/painting.html) and [SVG 2 Coordinate Systems](https://www.w3.org/TR/SVG2/coords.html). Retain authored declarations, origins, inheritance owners, and Source Spans while resolving a separate canonical record for `stroke-width`, `stroke-linecap`, `stroke-linejoin`, `stroke-miterlimit`, `stroke-dasharray`, `stroke-dashoffset`, and `vector-effect`.

Support the current unitless static slice. Resolve defaults, reject negative widths and miter limits, admit every SVG 2 cap and join keyword, duplicate odd dash lists, reject negative dash entries, collapse all-zero patterns to solid, normalize effective dash offsets by the even pattern sum, and admit only `none` and `non-scaling-stroke` vector effects. Use stroke paint, positive width, shape topology, and the resolved pattern to decide whether a property can affect Computed Appearance; source differences remain reportable even when their computed effect is neutral.

Use canonical stroke geometry for alignment, computed relations, parameter magnitudes, conservative painted bounds, renderer-input adaptation, and Cause Envelopes. Ordinary strokes are constructed in current user space; `non-scaling-stroke` is constructed in the element's host coordinate space after its cumulative transform. Exact continuous outline construction, path-length calibration, non-unitless lengths, markers, stylesheet cascade, and precise transform-aware localization remain separately guarded unless this item establishes them directly.

- [x] Focused pinned-renderer and Chromium fixtures establish or guard width, caps, joins, dashes, offsets, zero-width, and non-scaling-stroke behavior.
- [x] Current contracts, evaluation artifacts, compatibility/version identities, and the roadmap describe the implemented boundary.

## Notes
- A renderer-specific guard does not make canonical stroke semantics indeterminate. It limits only Rendered Evidence unless the renderer cannot produce an observation at all.
- Conservative painted bounds may overapproximate the ideal stroke shape, but they must not exclude any admitted cap or join contribution.
