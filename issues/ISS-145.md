## Description
Close the remaining Phase 6 localization gap for the bounded v1 profile. Transform events currently bypass already available cumulative painted bounds and therefore absorb every changed scene component. Incomplete same-document filter graphs also discard their resolved filter region even though that region is a sound upper bound for any unsupported primitive output, including SVG shadow primitives.

Use before/after cumulative painted bounds for transformed subjects and preserve the resolved filter region as a conservative fallback for incomplete referenced filter graphs. Retain the existing admitted stroke, marker, clip, mask, filter, and isolated-group bounds. Inputs without a sound bound remain partial and unlocalized rather than receiving guessed regions.

## Design constraints
- Region attachment may select only pixel-mask components intersecting the union of the subject's before/after cumulative painted bounds.
- Transformed bounds must include conservative stroke expansion, marker envelopes, and admitted clip/mask effect bounds already carried by `SourceSubject`.
- A referenced incomplete filter graph may use its resolved filter region as a conservative computed-bounds fallback because all primitive output is clipped to that region.
- Unsupported SVG shadow primitives remain source-only and rendered-unavailable; localization must not imply that their pixels, magnitude, or exact contribution were computed.
- Invalid or external CSS/SVG filter references without a resolved filter region remain unlocalized behind their existing Diagnostics.
- Keep insertion/deletion and movement side-specific bounds as the next separate roadmap item; this item may use their conservative union only.
- Do not add a severity, visibility, equality, or exact-cause claim.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] A transform event in a multi-change scene excludes disjoint pixel components outside its before/after transformed painted bounds.
- [x] Transformed geometry continues to retain all vacated and newly occupied components within the before/after union.
- [x] Admitted stroke, marker, clip, mask, filter, and isolated-group events retain nonempty bounded regions under their existing focused tests.
- [x] A changed unsupported same-document SVG shadow/filter primitive receives a conservative filter-region Difference Region while remaining partial, source-only, and rendered-unavailable.
- [x] Invalid or external filter references without a resolved resource region do not receive a fabricated bound.
- [x] Region Cause Envelopes and rendered magnitudes retain their existing guarantees and do not become stronger merely because a conservative bound exists.
- [x] Current contract, coverage, examples, package/version metadata, and the exact Phase 6 roadmap item are updated.
- [x] Focused and full relevant validation gates pass.
