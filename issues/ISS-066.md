## Description
Decide whether SVG Diff needs one scalar that totally orders geometry, paint, presence, raster, resource, and future perceptual differences.

## Design
Evaluate the scalar against the accepted raw-vector authority, same-domain tuple contract, human-label boundary, and future Impact Assessment requirements. Record an ADR that either adopts a fully specified scalar or rejects one while preserving a path to structured cross-domain assessment. Define concrete reopen evidence without silently inventing weights.

## Close Notes
Accepted ADR 0039: SVG Diff will not adopt a universal scalar that claims geometry, paint, presence, resource, raster, and future perceptual measurements are naturally commensurate. Named raw magnitude fields remain authoritative and `v1_domain_lexicographic` remains exact-domain only.

Defined a usable non-scalar path for future Impact Assessment: a structured, traceable policy result may apply calibrated rules or tiers, retain every raw input, expose which rules fired, and permit ties or incomparability while linking conclusions to events and differences. Human corpus categories and annotation tiers remain evaluation labels, not hidden engine weights or thresholds.

Scalar adoption may be reopened only when a concrete text-only Agent task demonstrates that a structured assessment with ties cannot solve it. Any proposal must then record policy identity, inputs, normalization, missing-value behavior, weights, thresholds, tie-breaking, representative calibration corpus, metric versions, sensitivity, and failures against the raw-vector baseline. No schema or output changed in this decision.

Verified generated interfaces and formatting, warning-clean native checks, all 103 MoonBit tests, canonical Schema examples and raw-vector derivation, evaluation metrics, reproducible benchmark pass/fail behavior, version identities, local Markdown links, and `git diff --check`.
