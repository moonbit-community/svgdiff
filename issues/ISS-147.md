## Description
Complete the source-input half of the accepted Influence Provenance model. Family analyzers already enumerate Changed Facts and conservatively populate `affected_subject_ids`, but Cause Envelope attachment currently consumes only the Changed Facts directly referenced by an event's Atomic Differences. Build one private token index from every Changed Fact to every rendered subject it may affect, resolve entity events through their before/after Subject Alignment identities, and retain every indexed token when constructing event-region candidates.

This item covers the graph edge from supported geometry, paint, resource, transform, viewport, use/instance, and structural inputs to rendered subjects. Transfer through group surfaces, clips, masks, filters, blending, and source-over compositing remains the next separate Phase 6 item.

## Design constraints
- Every direct event Changed Fact token remains a candidate even when it has no affected subject or alignment.
- Entity events query the union of before and after rendered identities from their entity-role Subject Alignment; insertion and deletion therefore retain the existing side's identity.
- Resource and relationship events without an entity alignment query the conservative `affected_subject_ids` of their direct facts.
- Token query order remains deterministic and preserves existing direct-candidate order before appending newly reached facts in report order.
- A fact that reaches only an unrelated subject must remain excluded from a complete disjoint event-region envelope.
- Partial reports and unavailable propagation rules continue to widen to the comparison Changed Fact universe and use `not_established`.
- Existing all-fact fallback for group opacity, blending, isolation, and stacking remains unchanged until the next roadmap item.
- Do not add public fields, claim tile-level provenance, exact contribution, visibility, or unique causality.
