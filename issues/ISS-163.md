## Description
Validate that Subject Alignment, Difference Regions, and Cause Envelopes remain sound across the declared supported set. Establish an aggregate milestone gate covering alignment cardinality and uncertainty, side-typed localization and non-contamination, complete actual-cause containment, conservative fallback, partial guarantee revocation, and closed report references.

This gate does not claim authoritative cross-document identity, exact pixel attribution, minimal causes, contribution weights, calibrated alignment confidence, or soundness outside the recorded support and coverage boundary.

## Design constraints
- Treat alignment as versioned correspondence evidence, never authored-ID identity or probability.
- Preserve one-to-one, insertion, deletion, split, merge, and exact equal-cardinality repeated-set behavior without inventing pairwise identity inside a tied class.
- Require deterministic tie-breaking and explicit ambiguity/confidence status.
- Preserve independent before/after bounds until region union so movement, insertion, and deletion retain the correct occupied sides.
- Prevent scene-wide or neighboring-subject pixels and facts from contaminating a subject-specific region or Cause Envelope when independence is established.
- Require every complete region's `sound_overapproximation` envelope to contain every independently declared actual changed cause; false positives remain allowed.
- Widen to all Changed Facts or revoke the guarantee whenever a sound propagation/localization rule is unavailable.
- Require partial paths to use `not_established` rather than making a complete causal claim.
- Validate supported family fan-out through mutation, feature-specific, curated-annotation, and adversarial evidence.
- Do not change product code, public APIs, Structured Report Schema, identities, dependencies, CLI behavior, fixtures, annotations, CI, or release assets unless a verified defect requires a scoped fix.
