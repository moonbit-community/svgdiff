## Description
Validate that renderer conformance dispositions and engine-owned coverage proof prevent false complete equality. Establish one traceable milestone gate from reproducible browser/pinned-renderer observations through every divergence disposition, encountered renderer Diagnostics, coverage-matrix status composition, and unsupported-input properties.

This is a safety-gate reconciliation. It does not declare the renderer browser-equivalent, turn an aggregate conformance percentage into support, remove a guard, accept a tolerance, or widen any complete-eligible feature slice.

## Design constraints
- Require one independent renderer-conformance profile identity across the production Comparison Profile, baseline, and dispositions.
- Require every measured baseline divergence to have exactly one reviewed Diagnostic, normalizer, or product-compositor disposition.
- Require admitted exact/normalized/composited fixtures to prove their stated complete behavior and guarded fixtures to execute their production Diagnostic.
- Keep renderer capability gaps encountered-only; an empty list is not global renderer support.
- Require centralized coverage proof to downgrade missing/inconsistent coverage declarations through `coverage_proof_incomplete`.
- Require unsupported self-comparisons to remain partial or failed with Diagnostics and limited/failed coverage cells.
- Preserve independently supported evidence when only one layer is renderer-limited.
- Correct stale maintenance documentation discovered by the audit without changing product behavior.
- Do not change renderer output, product code, public APIs, Structured Report Schema, identities, dependencies, CLI behavior, fixtures, baselines, CI, or release assets unless a verified defect requires a scoped fix.
