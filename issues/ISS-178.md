## Question

What should hover and persistent selection draw on each SVG preview when the report has a conservative before/after union region, side-typed bounds, exact changed pixels, unavailable rendered evidence, or no localizable region, and what claims may each visualization communicate?

## Notes

- 2026-07-18: Accepted two independent localization surfaces. Side Footprints show side-qualified subject or effect extents and preserve absence. Difference Outcome Surfaces show canvas-space observed or conservative result localization. Hover may show both only with distinct semantics, styling, legend, and text.
- 2026-07-18: Accepted an exact binary Observed Difference Mask as the target rendered-outcome surface. Bounds-only observed regions remain an explicit compatibility fallback, and conservative computed bounds use a distinct possible-extent presentation. The mask proves changed canonical pixels only, never visibility, unique cause, or per-event contribution.
- 2026-07-18: Accepted five explicit Side Footprint States per side: bounded, absent, proven empty, unavailable with reasons and Diagnostics, and not applicable. Bounded footprints remain conservative and carry method and coordinate-space identity; missing states never borrow the opposite side's bounds.
- 2026-07-18: Accepted Event-owned localization. Hovering an Atomic Difference shows its owning Event's shared surfaces with an explicit no-atomic-contribution label; presentation may not guess, split, recolor, or otherwise imply child-specific pixels without a separately versioned evidence surface.
- 2026-07-18: Accepted a fixed Localization Visual Grammar: side-specific solid labeled outlines, observed pixel fill, observed bounds-only outline, conservative dashed hatched bounds, and text-only unavailable states. Multiple region IDs remain separate; color is never the sole distinction and presentation does not create a synthetic region hull.
- 2026-07-18: Accepted a strict Localization Fallback Ladder: exact observed mask, observed bounds-only, conservative outcome bounds, then no outcome surface. Side Footprints stay independent and may be shown only as subject-location evidence. No guessed, opposite-side, root, host, nearest-region, or whole-canvas fallback is allowed without explicit report evidence.

## Close Notes

Resolved localization as two independent surfaces: side-qualified subject/effect footprints and canvas-space difference outcomes. The target observed outcome is an exact binary changed-pixel mask with a bounds-only compatibility fallback; conservative computed bounds remain visually and semantically distinct. Each side footprint uses explicit bounded, absent, proven-empty, unavailable, or not-applicable state. Atomic Difference hover reuses owning Event localization without implying child contribution. A fixed accessible visual grammar distinguishes surface sources and preserves every region ID. The fallback ladder ends honestly at no outcome surface, while independently available side footprints may still be shown as subject location only. These decisions require later report-versus-projection analysis because schema `1.44` does not serialize masks or typed side footprints.
