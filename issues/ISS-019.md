## Description
The unsupported-gradient signature records attributes only for self-closing `stop` elements. A paired `<stop></stop>` therefore loses its attributes and does not advance the stop index, allowing unsupported later-stop changes to retain complete coverage.

## Design
Canonicalize `Start(stop)` and `Empty(stop)` through the same attribute and index logic. Preserve the proven first-stop-color exclusion while retaining every other stop attribute in the conservative signature.
