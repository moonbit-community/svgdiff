# Resolve SVG viewports under one explicit Comparison Viewport

SVG Diff will resolve root and nested SVG viewport coordinate systems without giving the two inputs independent raster canvases. The caller-provided Comparison Profile remains the one common observation viewport. Root `width` and `height` are retained as intrinsic authored declarations, but they do not override that profile or separately resize before and after rendering. Automatic inference from matching intrinsic declarations remains deferred under ADR 0021.

The engine will own a strict adapter for `viewBox`, `preserveAspectRatio`, and nested `svg` `x`, `y`, `width`, and `height`. The implemented length slice accepts unitless values, `px`, and percentages against the nearest established viewport. It implements all nine alignments, `none`, `meet`, and `slice`, and interleaves each element's generic transform with its viewport mapping in root-to-leaf order. A nested `viewBox` establishes the child user-coordinate percentage basis; without one, the resolved nested viewport dimensions do.

A changed declaration is reported under `document.viewport` with its Source Span. The resulting leaf coordinate consequence is not a new magnitude system: it continues through `geometry.transform.cumulative_matrix` and the canonical translation, rotation, signed-scale, skew, or singular residual domains. This keeps the authored cause distinct from its potentially many visual outcomes while reusing the existing exact coordinate evidence and conservative Cause Envelope fan-out.

Invalid syntax, non-positive dimensions, and unsupported physical units emit `viewport_semantics_unsupported` and cannot produce complete equality. A valid mapping outside the browser-verified integer axis slice retains exact source and computed evidence but emits `renderer_viewport_raster_unproven`. Root `none`, meet, slice, and nested fixtures establish exact Chromium parity for the admitted slice. CSS sizing and cascade, object-bounding-box units, physical units, automatic Comparison Viewport inference, viewport clipping localization, and precise transform-aware bounds remain separate work.
